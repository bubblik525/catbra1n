Model provenance & limitations 65-region graph · trained readout ↗

Connectivity: published cat cortical area graph, sourced from NeuroData. The point cloud is an artistic layout, not anatomical coordinates. Input weights and propagation dynamics are engineered. Only the logistic output layer is trained.

227 training cases / 98 chronological held-out cases. Brier 0.1092 versus constant baseline 0.1119. One day of Pons data; 675 of 1,000 sampled tokens had insufficient records. This does not demonstrate a trading edge.

The model uses the first available hourly record and estimates the direction of the next available record. It does not forecast the current token price or a fixed 24-hour return. Old or truncated histories may not match training conditions.
